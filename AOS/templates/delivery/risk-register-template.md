## Risk Scoring Methodology

Every risk below is scored on this same 1-5 x 1-5 matrix — never an ad
hoc label. Score Likelihood and Impact independently, then read the
Risk Rating off the matrix; don't average the two numbers.

**Likelihood** — how likely is this risk to materialise absent further
mitigation, over the life of this engagement's governance model:

| Score | Descriptor | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Not expected to occur; no precedent at this organisation or comparable peers |
| 2 | Unlikely | Could occur, but no current indicator that it will |
| 3 | Possible | May occur; at least one contributing condition is already present |
| 4 | Likely | Expected to occur at least once within the engagement horizon |
| 5 | Almost Certain | Already occurring, or expected to occur multiple times per year |

**Impact** — the most severe of the four dimensions applies; a risk
that is Low financially but Severe regulatorily is scored Severe:

| Score | Descriptor | Financial | Regulatory | Operational | Reputational |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Negligible | No material cost | No reporting obligation triggered | No service disruption | No external visibility |
| 2 | Minor | Absorbable within budget | Internal escalation only | Localised, brief disruption | Internal only |
| 3 | Moderate | Material but not board-level | Regulator notification likely | Cross-team disruption | Client-visible |
| 4 | Major | Board-level financial exposure | Formal regulatory inquiry likely | Multi-system outage or failure | Trade press visibility |
| 5 | Severe | Existential to the business unit | Enforcement action, fine, or licence risk | Sustained multi-day outage | National media / public visibility |

**Risk Rating matrix** (Likelihood x Impact):

| | Impact 1 | Impact 2 | Impact 3 | Impact 4 | Impact 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Likelihood 5** | Medium | High | High | Critical | Critical |
| **Likelihood 4** | Medium | Medium | High | High | Critical |
| **Likelihood 3** | Low | Medium | Medium | High | High |
| **Likelihood 2** | Low | Low | Medium | Medium | High |
| **Likelihood 1** | Low | Low | Low | Medium | Medium |

**Target Closure** by rating (starting point — adjust to the client's
own risk appetite once confirmed): Critical — 30 days; High — 90 days;
Medium — 180 days; Low — next scheduled review.
