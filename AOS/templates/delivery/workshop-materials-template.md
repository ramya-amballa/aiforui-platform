## Session 1 — Opportunity

**Objective:** Catalogue current and planned AI use cases against
business intent.
**Materials:** AI inventory template (see Discovery Questionnaire),
use-case prioritisation matrix.
**Discussion prompts:**
- Which AI use case matters most to the business this year, and why?

## Session 2 — People

**Objective:** Assign a named owner for each use case and governance
decision (see `raci-template.md`).
**Materials:** RACI draft, org chart (client-provided).

## Session 3 — Evaluation

**Objective:** Apply a consistent risk model across use cases (see
`risk-register-template.md`), calibrated to impact and reversibility.
**Materials:** Risk register, readiness workbook scores.

## Session 4 — Response

**Objective:** Define escalation paths, approval gates and the
decision record.
**Materials:** Governance roadmap (Phase 3 — Govern), draft approval
workflow.

## Session 5 — Assurance

**Objective:** Establish the ongoing evidence trail and reporting
cadence.
**Materials:** Executive status report template, steering committee
pack template.
