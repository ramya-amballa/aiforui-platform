## Proposed Approach

Run through OPERA, adapted to DORA's ICT third-party and resilience
obligations:

- **Opportunity** — map DORA's obligations against current ICT
  third-party and resilience practice, not a generic commercial risk
  template
- **People** — assign ownership for ICT third-party risk and incident
  reporting distinctly, since DORA holds the regulated entity
  accountable regardless of the vendor
- **Evaluation** — assess sector-specific obligations: data residency,
  audit rights, incident notification timelines, concentration risk
- **Response** — build the contractual and monitoring structure DORA
  requires for critical ICT third parties
- **Assurance** — an evidence trail that tracks the obligation itself,
  not just the contract
