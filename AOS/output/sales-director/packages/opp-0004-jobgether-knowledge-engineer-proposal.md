<p><strong>This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, which manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Knowledge Engineer based in the United States.</strong></p>
<p>The Knowledge Engineer will play a pivotal role in building the knowledge infrastructure and AI-powered systems that support business operations and customer experiences. In this role, you will design and maintain trusted knowledge repositories, intelligent AI agents, and automated workflows that help teams access accurate information and streamline everyday tasks. Working across departments such as Support, Customer Success, Finance, Product, and Engineering, you will transform fragmented business knowledge into structured, AI-accessible resources. You will also develop secure integrations and intelligent automation solutions that enable AI systems to perform routine activities while maintaining appropriate human oversight. By combining expertise in AI engineering, data architecture, and knowledge management, you will help establish scalable and reliable AI capabilities across the organization. </div><h4>Accountabilities:</h4><div>

Design, develop, and maintain a centralized, trusted knowledge infrastructure that makes product documentation, technical resources, business policies, operational procedures, and training materials accessible to AI systems.
Consolidate and organize knowledge from multiple platforms, including Google Drive, Confluence, Jira, Salesforce, Snowflake, Omni, HubSpot, Zendesk, Highspot, and Slack, ensuring that each source has clear ownership and scheduled review dates.
Collaborate with Data and Business Insights teams to make existing business data AI-ready by providing curated, read-only Snowflake views and standardized metric definitions that align with established reporting systems.
Build, deploy, and maintain AI agents and automated workflows for business functions such as customer support ticket triage, renewal research, accounts payable processing, and software reviews.
Develop and manage connectors, APIs, Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, and automation integrations that allow AI agents to interact securely with business systems while enforcing appropriate permissions, human approvals, and audit trails.
Create automated pipelines that identify changes in documentation, business systems, and operational processes, ensuring that knowledge repositories, AI agents, and workflows remain accurate and up to date.
Structure and optimize information for AI retrieval through taxonomy development, metadata management, content chunking, tagging, embeddings, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) techniques.
Establish evaluation frameworks and quality assurance processes to measure AI agent accuracy, detect outdated or conflicting information, and minimize hallucinations before and after deployment.
Maintain a centralized registry of approved AI connectors, prompts, plugins, reusable skills, and workflows to ensure that AI solutions remain secure, standardized, and company-owned.
Implement and manage access controls, data permissions, and security practices to protect sensitive information and ensure compliance with internal IT and security requirements.
Document technical architecture, workflows, integration standards, and operational processes while staying informed about emerging AI technologies and industry best practices.
Monitor AI usage, operational costs, adoption, and feedback to identify knowledge gaps, improve answer quality, and prioritize future enhancements.
Partner with subject-matter experts across Support, Customer Success, Finance, Accounting, Revenue Operations, Product, and Engineering to capture institutional knowledge, map business processes, and establish content ownership.
Collaborate with training teams, Product Marketing, and departmental AI champions to transform product documentation, customer support resources, and training materials into AI-accessible knowledge for internal teams and external customers.
Integrate AI-powered tools into employee and customer training programs to improve knowledge sharing, self-service capabilities, and operational efficiency.
